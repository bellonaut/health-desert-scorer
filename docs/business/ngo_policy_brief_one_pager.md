# Health Desert Scorer (Nigeria)
## One-Page Outreach Brief for NGOs, PHC Teams, and Clinics

### 30-second summary
Health Desert Scorer is a map-first planning tool that helps partners identify Nigerian LGAs with higher relative barriers to healthcare access. It combines access signals (facility availability, distance, connectivity, and child health proxy indicators) into a transparent prioritization score so outreach, staffing, and service expansion decisions can start from evidence.

### Why this matters now
- Program teams are asked to do more with constrained budgets.
- Planning often depends on fragmented spreadsheets and manual judgement.
- Teams need faster, defensible prioritization for outreach and service delivery.

### What this tool does
- Ranks LGAs by relative access barrier intensity.
- Lets users switch focus across all-risk, child mortality, facility access, connectivity, and 5km coverage.
- Supports LGA search, side-by-side comparison, and export-ready outputs.
- Maintains ethical framing: this is for planning support, not diagnosis.

### Who benefits
- NGOs and implementation partners: sharper geographic targeting.
- State PHC and policy teams: transparent prioritization for allocation discussions.
- Clinic networks and outreach teams: clearer sequencing for community campaigns.
- Research groups: reproducible and interpretable spatial risk hypotheses.
