# Partner Targets, Niche, and Revenue Plan
## Health Desert Scorer (Nigeria)

## 1) Can this app get you paid?
Yes, if you sell it as a **decision-support service for health program execution**, not as a generic map app.

The strongest path is:
- paid pilot + onboarding,
- then annual software access and support,
- then add custom analytics/reporting for funders and research teams.

Do not position this as "another dashboard." Position it as:
- faster microplanning,
- better geographic targeting,
- and auditable prioritization logic.

## 2) Your niche (recommended)
**Niche:** last-mile public health planning at LGA level for Nigeria (starting with maternal/child health, immunization, and outreach operations).

Why this niche fits you:
- You already have Nigeria LGA coverage and relevant risk features.
- You already have a usable, shareable product interface.
- You already have a model card and metrics for credibility.

### Beachhead customer profile
- Program managers in NGOs and implementation partners.
- State PHC planners and digital health units.
- Clinic networks running outreach in underserved catchments.

### Core value proposition
"We reduce planning time and improve targeting quality by turning fragmented spatial health signals into a ranked, explainable, and exportable LGA action list."

## 3) Offer ladder and pricing (example starting points)
Use ranges first, then tune after first 2-3 paid pilots.

### Offer A: Paid pilot (12 weeks)
- Scope: 1 state, 10-20 active users, onboarding + weekly support
- Deliverables: configured app workspace, training, exports, pilot evaluation memo
- Price: NGN 4,000,000 to NGN 12,000,000

### Offer B: Annual program subscription
- Scope: hosted access, quarterly data refresh, support SLAs
- Tier 1 (single-state): NGN 8,000,000 to NGN 18,000,000 per year
- Tier 2 (multi-state): NGN 20,000,000 to NGN 60,000,000 per year

### Offer C: Research and donor analytics package
- Scope: custom outputs, methods appendix, reproducible exports, presentations
- Price: NGN 3,000,000 to NGN 15,000,000 per engagement

### Fast way to close first revenue
- Ask for a paid discovery workshop first (NGN 500,000 to NGN 2,000,000).
- Convert workshop into a fixed pilot proposal within 7 days.

## 5) How to make it happen (180-day execution plan)
## Days 0-30: tighten the product for buyers
1. Lock a "pilot edition" package (scope, timeline, deliverables, price).
2. Add explicit data freshness and uncertainty labels in the app.
3. Prepare three assets:
- one-page brief,
- 10-slide deck,
- 2-page pilot statement of work template.

## Days 31-90: run focused outbound
1. Build a pipeline of 30 target organizations from the list above.
2. Send tailored outreach to 10/week.
3. Run 8-12 demos.
4. Close 2 paid discovery workshops.
5. Convert at least 1 workshop to a paid 12-week pilot.

## Days 91-180: convert pilots to recurring revenue
1. Deliver pilot report with quantified value:
- planning cycle time reduction,
- targeting precision improvements,
- adoption and repeat usage.
2. Offer annual subscription immediately after pilot close.
3. Publish one case study and one technical note to improve trust and inbound leads.
